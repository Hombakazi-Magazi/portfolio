ABOUT BIKINI BEACH BOOKS

The first thing that will probably grab your attention, as you enter this bookstore, is the large cursive sign, ‘Bikini Beach Books’, at the entrance, a token of 1950s America. As you enter, you will be greeted with writings from all spectrums of genres, guiding you to shelves installed with bodice rippers, Westerns, light readings as well as magazines, as you step further into the bookstore, nothing can prepare you for the exploration that lies ahead. With books starting at 50c, not only will your wallet probably not feel the footprint of you being in the store, but the knowledge and journey taken from interacting with the literature, will last you a lifetime You can think of it, as, ‘travel starting at 50c’, to another land, time, and even universe! With fictional writings labelled alphabetically, whilst, non-fictional grouped by genre, legend has it that our bookstore has been cleverly designed to assist you, our visitor, to search and find, which we keep our promise, you will. Located on Beach Road, Gordan’s Bay, behind three Goliath-sized palms, we assure you this is a journey that you will not want to have never taken. As quoted, “An iconic staple of the Gordon’s Bay beach that’s been buying and selling books for over 25 years” (Anon, n.d.)
