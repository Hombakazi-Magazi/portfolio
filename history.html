OUR STORY

Bikini Beach Books is owned and founded by Anton Grick. In 1969, Anton Grick came to South Africa from Austria, in pursuit of a career in mechanical engineering. In 1979, he went on to buy property in Gordons Bay – an old boarding house known as Oleander, which he renovated to become Bikini Beach Books. Due to his love of literature and reading from a young, he said, it was only natural that he would one day trade in books. The 1950’s America-styled, “Bikini Books’ sign, was put up in 1994, reflecting Anton’s handwriting. As he puts it in his own words, “I came upon the idea of creating a great bookshop in Gordon’s Bay and committed my thoughts and designs to paper while on a visit to Sun City and thereafter Las Vegas, a city full of bright lights and peculiar American-style neon signs”. Seven years later the “Bikini” in beach books was added. Starting with one table of books, Bikini Beach Books, has since grown, Anton became prominent for his second handbooks, as well as his knowledge about the book trade. Bikini Beach Books is ladened with books donated by some people in Gordon’s Bay, although a plethora of the literature was acquired from dealers, welfare organizations, traders as well as importers in Cape Town, hence, the vast genres, in Bikini Beach Books today. Bikini Beach Books is now known as one of Gordon’s Bay landmarks. Bikini Beach Books is filled with books that cater to a vast audience, whether you are searching for a novel to read until you fall asleep or any other form of literature, Bikini Beach Books is sure to cater. Bikini Beach Books is designed for wanderers, as Anton happily states, “The pleasure of reading is not only sitting down with your favourite books or author, but also in searching and discovering and sniffing and meandering through the bookshop until you find a book that moves your soul”. Whether it is fiction, non-fiction, children’s books, self-help, history, geography, space travel, politics, law, or hundreds of biographies, just to name a few, Bikini Beach Books is sure to exceed your expectations. Anton prides himself in informing himself as much as he can about different literature, as he states, “You can always learn and read about new things”. Bikini Beach Books can be described as a treasure chest, with gold and hidden gems ready to be discovered, and if you get lost, Anton and his team, Beulah, and Martha – two book-savvy assistants – will be ready like a true treasure map to help you find your way (Aswegen, 2017).
